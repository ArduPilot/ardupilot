--- Annotations (2026-08-15, tree af8ea06af3+, EKF single-precision + fast sqrtf) ---

Third capture today. Changes since the morning's:

1. sqrtf() 1.1042 -> 0.2756 us/call (4.0x): AP_HAL_Zephyr/system.cpp now
   aliases sqrtf to libgcc's __ieee754_sqrtf - the LX7 FPU hardware
   sqrt-assist sequence (SQRT0.S + 11x MADDN.S, 35 instrs) that was in the
   SDK all along; picolibc's generic C sqrtf was winning the link. Now
   FASTER than both the classic-ESP32 ROM version (0.43) and an F4's
   hardware VSQRT path (0.29). sqrt() (double) dropped identically because
   picolibc routes it through the float path.

2. Earlier same day: EKF single precision (132.2 -> 22.8 us) and the
   wrap-extended micros() - see the per-change notes in git.

QIO flash mode: TRIED AND REVERTED. Passing a real "qio" image header
through (submodule Kconfig.esptool patch) boot-looped the board - with
ESP_SIMPLE_BOOT nothing sets the flash QE bit before the ROM switches
modes; Zephyr's silent qio->dio header downgrade is what keeps the board
booting, not a bug. Runtime QE-bit support is the future path to the
remaining EKF gap vs classic ESP32 (22.8 vs 16.2 us = XIP fetch: DIO vs
QIO plus 16 KB vs 32 KB icache; the bigger icache also costs 16 KB of the
~33 KB free internal DRAM, so it waits on a .dram0.noinit audit - 237 KB).

Still open (pre-existing):
- delay_microseconds(1) = 11.3 us/call against the 1 MHz kernel tick.
- fdiv 0.27 us is already the FPU divide-assist sequence in libgcc
  (div0.s/divn.s/maddn.s verified) - the F4's 0.12 single-instruction VDIV
  is silicon we cannot match; only ~call-overhead is trimmable.
