  cpu_used% = total CPU the SPI path consumed (thread + ISR).
  isr_cpu%  = the part of it spent INSIDE interrupt handlers.
  irq/xfer counts events; isr_cpu% is what they COST - a high
  irq/xfer with a low isr_cpu% is not what is binding the CPU.

  the bottom line; us/xfer may rise. Run this on a CPU-driver
  build and a DMA build and diff the tables.
