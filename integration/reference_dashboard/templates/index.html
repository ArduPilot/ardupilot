AUV Pool-Tuning Guide
Everything you need while tuning in the water — read top to bottom the first time.
0 · Golden safety rules
  • Disarm before touching the vehicle. The big red EMERGENCY KILL disarms at 20 Hz for 3 s — know where it is.
  • Always start with a propeller-clear test on the Thrusters tab before arming.
  • Tune one axis at a time. Disable the others (Roll/Pitch/Yaw/Depth enable toggles) so you only feel what you're changing.
  • Make small changes — the ± buttons step by 10% and apply instantly. Commit to disk only once it feels right.
1 · How the control stack flows

It's a cascaded ArduPilot-style controller:

IMU ─► Attitude Controller ─► Motor Mixer ─► ESP32 ─► 8 ESCs
        (angle P → rate PID)     (6-DOF allocate)   (PWM)
  • Outer loop (Angle P): turns an angle error into a target rotation rate. Gain = ang_*_p.
  • Inner loop (Rate PID): turns rate error into a motor effort (−1…1). Gains = rate_*_p/i/d.
  • Mixer: maps the 6 efforts (roll, pitch, yaw, vertical, fwd, lat) onto 8 thrusters, then PWM.
2 · Before you get in the pool
  • Click Launch and confirm all nodes go green (IMU Hz > 0, thruster Hz > 0).
  • Wait for the gyro-bias dot to go green (~2 s of stillness) — you can't arm before it.
  • On the Thrusters tab, spin each motor briefly and confirm direction; fix with REV.
  • Set sane PWM limits in the launch file (pwm_min/pwm_max, default 1300/1700) — start gentle.
3 · Calibration sequence (order matters)
  1. Reset Gyro Bias — hold the vehicle still; re-zeros gyro drift.
  2. Zero Pose (Level) — hold the vehicle level/neutral, press it. Current orientation becomes 0,0,0.
  3. Capture Reference — sets the current heading/attitude as the hold target.
  4. Reset PID States — wipes integrators/derivatives any time the bot "remembers" an old push.
4 · The tuning workflow

Do Roll first, then Pitch (they stabilize the bot), then Yaw, then Depth.

  1. Enable only the axis you're tuning. Arm. Master-enable on.
  2. Inner rate loop first. Raise rate_*_p until the bot feels stiff/responsive; if it buzzes/oscillates fast, back off ~20%.
  3. Add rate_*_i slowly to kill steady-state lean. Too much → slow wobble/overshoot. Watch the I term — it should settle near 0, not keep growing (windup).
  4. Add a touch of rate_*_d only if you need to damp overshoot. Too much → the D term gets noisy/jittery.
  5. Outer angle loop: raise ang_*_p until it returns to target briskly without overshooting.
  6. Nudge the vehicle by hand and watch it recover on the Plots tab. Iterate. Commit Gains to Disk when happy.
  7. CRITICAL: Do not move to the next axis until the current one is rock solid.
5 · Gain cheat-sheet
Gain Does Too low Too high
ang_*_p How hard it drives back to the target angle Sluggish, droops away from target Overshoots / bounces around target
rate_*_p Stiffness against rotation Soft, slow, mushy Fast buzz / high-pitch oscillation
rate_*_i Removes steady-state lean Holds at an offset, never centers Slow wobble, windup, overshoot
rate_*_d Damps overshoot Overshoots before settling Jittery, amplifies gyro noise
rate_*_imax Caps the I term (anti-windup) Can't overcome bias Lets windup build up
max_rp_output / max_yaw_output Decoupled roll/pitch and yaw effort clamps Weak authority Slams motors to full saturation
6 · Reading the Live PID Terms

On the Dashboard tab, pick an axis in the tuning panel — the Live PID Terms box shows ERR / P / I / D / OUT and a bar of each term's share. The Plots tab graphs P/I/D over time.

  • I keeps climbing and never returns to ~0 → windup. Lower ki or imax, or press Reset PID States.
  • D bar is large and jumpy → derivative noise. Lower kd.
  • P dominates and OUT oscillates steadily → kp too high.
  • OUT pinned at the clamp → you're saturating; reduce gains or raise the effort limit cautiously.
7 · Depth tuning
  • Convention is Z-up: deeper = more negative. "Set Ref to current" gives zero error at your current depth.
  • Outer depth_p → target vertical velocity; inner depth_vel_p/i → thrust. Tune inner first, like the rate loop.
  • depth_ff is buoyancy trim — a constant vertical push to offset positive/negative buoyancy.
  • Don't tune depth on the bench — out of water the error/integral wind up. Do it in the pool.
8 · Thruster direction & weight tuning
  • Direction (NRM/REV): on the Thrusters tab, drive a motor; if it pushes the wrong way, hit REV.
  • Weight: 0–2 multiplier per motor to balance mismatched thrusters. Drive opposing pairs at equal PWM; trim the stronger one down until there's no drift/spin.
  • Both direction and weight persist to disk and reload on next launch.
9 · Control modes
  • Hold — auto-stabilize at the captured attitude/depth (your default for tuning).
  • Setpoint — command explicit roll/pitch/yaw/position targets.
  • Throttle — direct stick efforts (Throttle Control tab); RPY still stabilized.
10 · Troubleshooting
Symptom Likely cause / fix
Won't arm Gyro bias not ready — hold still until the dot is green.
Motors dead when armed Master-enable off, or the axis is disabled, or output clamp at 0.
Slow constant lean Add rate_*_i; check Zero Pose was captured level.
Fast buzz/oscillation Lower rate_*_p (then kd).
Drifts/spins with no command Thruster direction or weight imbalance — re-check on Thrusters tab.
Yaw spins the long way at ±180° Expected wrap handling — re-capture reference near your working heading.
Everything froze mid-run IMU timeout failsafe (neutral output). Check the IMU link / Hz.
